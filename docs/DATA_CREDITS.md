# Data credits and acknowledgments

The app shows these on its Library page (`GET /api/v1/credits`; source of truth: `backend/services/credits.py`). The acknowledgment sentences are copied word for word from IRSA's pages, read on 2026-11-15. Do not paraphrase them.

**SPHEREx** (https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/Missions/spherex.html): "This publication makes use of data products from the Spectro-Photometer for the History of the Universe, Epoch of Reionization and Ices Explorer (SPHEREx), which is a joint project of the Jet Propulsion Laboratory and the California Institute of Technology, and is funded by the National Aeronautics and Space Administration."

**NASA/IPAC Infrared Science Archive** (https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/ack.html): "This research has made use of the NASA/IPAC Infrared Science Archive, which is funded by the National Aeronautics and Space Administration and operated by the California Institute of Technology."

**SIMBAD / CDS** (names and proper motions; SIMBAD user's guide): "This research has made use of the SIMBAD database, operated at CDS, Strasbourg, France." Reference: Wenger et al. 2000, A&AS 143, 9.

**Astropy** (https://www.astropy.org/acknowledging.html): "This research made use of Astropy, a community-developed core Python package and an ecosystem of tools and resources for astronomy (Astropy Collaboration 2013, 2018, 2022)."

**Datasets** (https://www.ipac.caltech.edu/dois/irsa), author "SPHEREx Team": SPHEREx Data Release 1 (DR1) Spectral Images, doi:10.26131/IRSA678; SPHEREx Quick Release Spectral Images QR3, doi:10.26131/IRSA662.

**Canonical papers** (IRSA's SPHEREx page): Bock et al. (2026) mission; Akeson et al. (2026) pipeline; Crill et al. (2025) sky simulator; Korngut et al. (2026) instrument and calibration.

**Reference only**: WiseView (Caselden et al. 2018, ASCL 1806.004, https://github.com/backyardworlds/wiseview) inspired the blink workflow. It uses WISE data, and no WiseView code is used.

**AI**: Navi's explanations are written by Google Gemini models from measurements the app computed; they are not scientific findings. Processed pictures and stacks are derived products, not NASA observations.
